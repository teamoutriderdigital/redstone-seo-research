Recommendation

  • No SEO risk. The hyphenated domain is not cannibalizing, not serving content, and has zero search visibility. It is completely inert from an SEO perspective.
  • 301 redirect it to redstonemanufacturing.com. This is the safest action — it captures any direct-type traffic from users who guess the hyphenated version, and consolidates any future link equity.
  • Keep the registration. Don't let the domain expire — a competitor or squatter could register it and create a confusing brand signal. The registration cost is minimal insurance.
  • Cancel the A2 Hosting. Once the 301 redirect is configured at the DNS/registrar level (either via the registrar's forwarding feature or a simple Cloudflare redirect rule), there is no need to pay for hosting on this domain. The redirect can be done without a hosting account.
  • Timeline: Low priority. Can be done any time, but ideally before the new site launches to ensure clean redirect mapping.
Bottom line: redstone-manufacturing.com is a dead domain with an A2 Hosting placeholder. Zero rankings, zero traffic, zero cannibalization risk. 301 redirect to redstonemanufacturing.com, keep the registration, cancel the hosting. 5-minute fix.

06  Xometry Learning/Tools pages

Should we build these? Eric's yes/no ask.

Eric at 36:43: "I want Jack to look at should we build these or not. I just want to know yes or no." Here is the answer.

YES: Comparison content

  • "Die casting vs injection molding" (vol 170, KD 1)
  • "Die casting vs metal injection molding" (vol 70, KD 2)
  • Process comparison pages rank well because buyers need decision support
  • Low KD = easy wins
  • AI Overview present but FAQ schema earns citation

YES: FAQ schema pages

  • FAQ schema on every service page targets AI Overview citations
  • Goal: get cited in AI Overviews, not compete with them
  • Xometry does this extensively on their knowledge base
  • Low effort per page (5-7 Q&A accordion)
  • Doubles as on-page engagement signal

NO: Pure informational material specs

  • 49% of keywords have AI Overview in our dataset
  • Material spec queries ("what is aluminum die casting") = exactly what AI answers directly
  • Eric saw this: "our entire SEO just died" for this content type
  • CTR drop of 60%+ where AI Overviews appear (per Adam's parting note)
  • High content investment ($1K+ per page with diagrams) for diminishing returns
The answer: HYBRID.

Yes on comparison content (die casting vs investment casting, casting vs forging) and FAQ schema on every service page. These are low-KD, low-cost, and position Redstone as the decision-support resource buyers need.

Yes on calculator/tools if Redstone has proprietary data (e.g., landed-cost calculator for Mexico vs China). Interactive tools earn backlinks and are hard for AI to replicate.

No on Xometry-style pure informational material specification pages. AI Overviews now answer "what is aluminum die casting" directly. The 49% AI Overview rate in our keyword set confirms this. Redstone's existing material guides (st37, ss400, adc12) should be preserved (they still drive ~2K sessions/mo) but we should not invest in net-new material spec content. Instead, redirect that content budget toward commercial-intent service pages where AI Overviews are absent.
